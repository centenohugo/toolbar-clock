# Privacy policy: Minimalistic Toolbar Clock

Last updated: 2026-10-30

Minimalistic Toolbar Clock does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

- **Settings and time zones** (the zones you add, their labels, the 12/24 h format and the icon text color) are stored with `chrome.storage.sync`. This is the browser's own storage, tied to your Chrome or Brave profile and synced by your browser account if you have sync turned on. The extension never sends them anywhere else.
- **Time** is computed on your device from your system clock and the time zone database built into the browser. No network request is made.
- **Permissions**: `storage` keeps your settings; `alarms` redraws the toolbar icon at the start of every minute.

The extension contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or remote code.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/centenohugo/toolbar-clock/issues
